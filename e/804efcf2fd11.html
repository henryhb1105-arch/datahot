原文

Azure Databricks is a Visionary-level sponsor at Microsoft Ignite 2026, November 17–20 at Moscone Center in San Francisco. You'll find us at Booth #5271R in The Hub, in sessions featuring Azure Databricks and at The Social @ Canopy Hotel for executive meetings.

This is a collaborative post from Databricks and Microsoft. We thank Alexandra Harris, Sr. Product Marketing Manager at Microsoft, for their contributions.

This year we're showing how to Build, Govern and Run AI that's yours on Azure Databricks, with Genie in Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Copilot Cowork, all governed by Unity Catalog. Our sessions and demos focus on helping organizations move from AI pilots to AI that runs in production.

Microsoft Ignite 2026 at a glance

  • Dates: November 17–20, 2026
  • Venue: Moscone Center, San Francisco
  • Azure Databricks booth: #5271R in The Hub, November 17–19
  • Executive meetings: The Social @ Canopy Hotel, November 17–18, 9:00 AM–5:00 PM PT
  • Featured session: Build governed AI that understands your business context, Wednesday, November 18, 2:15 PM PT

Why visit Azure Databricks at Microsoft Ignite 2026

Microsoft Ignite 2026 is your chance to see governed AI on Azure Databricks working in the Microsoft tools your teams already use. Request a meeting with our experts, visit our booth in The Hub and join our sessions to see how Azure Databricks helps you build, govern and run AI grounded in your own business context.

How to meet Databricks executives at Microsoft Ignite 2026

Meet with Databricks leaders at The Social @ Canopy Hotel: Tuesday, November 17 – Wednesday, November 18 | 9:00 AM–5:00 PM PT | Steps from Moscone Center

Book a private meeting with Databricks executives and experts to discuss your data and AI priorities. No Ignite pass required. [Request a Meeting]

Databricks + AMD + Replit Customer Reception: Tuesday, November 17 | 6:00–8:00 PM PT | Mariposa, San Francisco

Connect with peers and leaders from Databricks, AMD and Replit to exchange ideas on data, AI and building with agents. [RSVP for the Databricks + AMD + Replit reception]

We look forward to seeing you in San Francisco.

Azure Databricks sessions at Microsoft Ignite 2026

Our session lineup offers learning opportunities for business leaders, data professionals, IT leaders and developers. Join us to learn about governed AI, agents, open data and interoperability across Microsoft products.

Azure Databricks for government cloud: Modern data and AI, delivered

Tuesday, November 17 | 2:00 PM PT | Moscone South, The Hub, Lightning Talk 5 Speaker: Shivani Tripathi (Microsoft)

Learn how Azure Databricks brings the latest data, analytics and AI capabilities to Azure Government, helping agencies speed up mission outcomes while meeting security, compliance and sovereignty requirements. This lightning talk covers modern lakehouse architecture, AI-powered analytics and governance, and how public sector organizations can use the newest Azure Databricks capabilities to make better data-driven decisions.

Fine-tune NVIDIA Nemotron models with Azure Databricks AI Runtime

Wednesday, November 18 | 1:20 PM PT | The Aviary, Lightning Talks Speakers: Prasad Kona, Saira Qureshi (Databricks)

Learn how to turn an open model into one that understands your business data. Join Azure Databricks and NVIDIA for a hands-on walkthrough of post-training with AI Runtime and NVIDIA Nemotron models. Using receipt extraction as a multimodal example, see how to prepare labeled data, adapt a model with NVIDIA NeMo, and compare base and fine-tuned results, along with serverless GPU infrastructure and key security, governance and operational considerations.

Scale agentic AI and turn data into autonomous action

Wednesday, November 18 | 1:45 PM PT | AMC Metreon, Level 3, Theater 11 Speakers: Diego Jarne (PwC), Bernhard Pfirrmann (Nokia), Eric Tranby (CareQuest Institute for Oral Health), Corrinne Walkaus (Markel)

Many organizations have AI pilots, but fewer have AI that runs reliably in production. Success depends on trusted, governed data. Learn how Azure OpenAI, Azure Databricks and modern lakehouse architectures help organizations build agentic AI at scale, with real-world examples from life sciences, insurance and telecommunications.

Build governed AI that understands your business context

Wednesday, November 18 | 2:15 PM PT | Moscone South | The Hub, Theater F Speakers: Robert Saxby (Databricks), Michel Porter (EY)

Hear from Robert Saxby of Databricks and Michel Porter, EY Global Assurance Analytics Leader, as they share how EY's assurance practice uses Azure Databricks to ingest, transform and govern billions of records for complex audit engagements, and what comes next. The session closes with a live demo of Genie in Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Copilot Cowork, showing how Azure Databricks helps AI systems understand your business context, identify authoritative sources, and deliver trusted, governed answers grounded in your organization's data.

What's new and what's next in Azure Storage

Wednesday, November 18 | 3:00 PM PT | AMC Metreon, Level 3, Theater 15 Speakers: David Meyer (Databricks); Aung Oo, Vybava Ramadoss (Microsoft)

Get up to speed on the latest Azure Storage innovations across object, file, block and data movement solutions. Databricks SVP of Product David Meyer joins Microsoft product leaders to share how Azure Databricks works with both Azure Data Lake Storage (ADLS) and OneLake, and what that means for customers bringing their data to AI. Learn how new capabilities help organizations improve performance, strengthen resiliency, simplify operations, optimize costs and build AI-ready data foundations.

Manage and secure your AI agents at enterprise scale with Agent 365

Wednesday, November 18 | 4:15 PM PT | Moscone West, Level 2, Room 2006 Speakers: David Meyer (Databricks); Nirav Shah, Ray Smith, Caroline Stanford (Microsoft)

Move from AI agent experimentation to adoption at scale. Explore how Microsoft Agent 365, the control plane for agents, helps organizations operate and scale agents with visibility, built-in governance and enterprise-grade security across their AI ecosystem. See what's new with Agent 365 and learn how to speed up agent adoption while keeping trust, compliance and operational control.

Powering AI with Azure Databricks, Foundry, Fabric, and Copilot

Thursday, November 19 | 10:15 AM PT | Moscone West, Level 2, Room 2009 Speakers: David Meyer (Databricks), Anavi Nahar (Microsoft)

AI is only as powerful as the data behind it. Join Databricks SVP of Product David Meyer and Microsoft's Anavi Nahar to explore how Azure Databricks powers AI experiences through integrations with OneLake, Microsoft Fabric, Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Copilot Studio, Power BI, Microsoft Teams and Microsoft 365. Learn how to build a governed, open data estate with Unity Catalog, bring trusted enterprise data to copilots and AI agents, and speed up business outcomes with shared governance and interoperability.

More Microsoft Ignite 2026 sessions on AI, agents and data

These Microsoft-led sessions cover AI, agents, infrastructure and data across Microsoft products.

Observability at every layer for every role

Wednesday, November 18 | 2:00 PM - 2:30 PM PT | Moscone West | Level 1, Exhibit Hall Speakers: Hayete Gallot, Charles Lamanna (Microsoft)

As AI and agents scale, visibility, trust, and security become business imperatives. Join Charles Lamanna, Executive Vice President, Copilot, Agents, and Platform, and Hayete Gallot, Executive Vice President, Microsoft Security, to explore how observability at every layer of the stack helps organizations understand where AI is being used, how agents are operating, what risks are emerging, and whether investments are delivering results. See how organizations can turn visibility into action to scale AI with confidence.

What's new in Work IQ

Tuesday, November 17 | 2:00 PM PT | Moscone West, Level 2, Room 2001 Speakers: Amanda Silver, Tolga Kilicli (Microsoft); Vibhor Chhabra (OpenAI)

Discover the latest innovations in Work IQ. Learn how Work IQ is expanding organizational intelligence through richer context across Microsoft 365 data such as emails and chats, advanced retrieval and grounding experiences, and unified intelligence endpoints. Get an inside look at new capabilities, integration patterns and the vision for powering smarter agents and personalized AI across Microsoft products.

Enterprise Frontier Transformation: Defining a New Era of Growth

Wednesday, November 18 | 11:30 AM PT | Moscone South, Level 2, Room 211 Speakers: Stephen Boyle, Matt Kerner (Microsoft)

AI is reshaping how enterprises operate, innovate and create value, opening new opportunities for partners to lead the next wave of transformation. Explore why building solutions, services and practice capabilities on the Microsoft AI platform helps customers become Frontier organizations, with trust, intelligence and model choice at the foundation, and learn how to accelerate AI sales and adoption together.

Cobalt 200: Delivering Azure's best performance and efficiency

Wednesday, November 18 | 3:00 PM PT | Moscone West, Level 3, Room 3005 Speakers: Amar Dhamdhere, Pat Stemen (Microsoft); Vadim Filanovsky (OpenAI)

Compute efficiency has become a first-order design constraint for modern workloads. Get an inside look at how Cobalt 200 is engineered end to end, from silicon through systems, to deliver price-performance on Azure. Learn about the Cobalt roadmap, what's new in Cobalt 200 virtual machines (VMs), and hear how Azure customers are turning the performance gains in Cobalt 200 into business value.

Seismic shift: Turning subsurface data into decisions

Wednesday, November 18 | 4:20 PM PT | Moscone South, The Hub, Theater C Speakers: Upendra Singh, Jake Umbriaco (Microsoft)

Subsurface decisions carry the most risk and the biggest prize, and both start with accessible, governed data. Learn how Azure Data Manager for Energy connects seismic, well and reservoir data with Microsoft Fabric and Azure Databricks while enabling advanced analytics, AI and reservoir modeling at scale. Discover how organizations turn complex energy data into intelligence that powers analytics, copilots and agents, accelerating decisions and reducing uncertainty.

What to see at the Azure Databricks booth (#5271R)

Visit Booth #5271R in The Hub, November 17–19, to explore live demos and talk with our product experts.

Highlights include:

  • Genie in Microsoft Copilot Cowork: See how the Azure Databricks Genie plugin brings governed answers from your Azure Databricks data directly into Copilot Cowork, without leaving the flow of work.
  • Genie One in Microsoft Teams and Microsoft Excel: Ask business questions in natural language and get trusted answers where your teams already work.
  • OneLake and Unity Catalog mirroring: See how Unity Catalog mirroring brings OneLake data into Azure Databricks without copying it.
  • Lakeflow: Build, automate and manage data pipelines from ingestion to insight.

You can also find Azure Databricks experts at the Azure Databricks Ask the Experts kiosk on the Expo floor. Our team of Azure Databricks subject matter experts will be available to discuss your data strategy and answer your questions about Azure Databricks.

We look forward to seeing you in San Francisco.

FAQs

When and where is Microsoft Ignite 2026?

Microsoft Ignite 2026 takes place November 17–20, 2026, at the Moscone Center in San Francisco. Azure Databricks is attending as a Visionary-level sponsor, with sessions, booth demos and executive meetings throughout the week.

Where can I find Azure Databricks at Microsoft Ignite 2026?

Visit Azure Databricks at Booth #5271R in The Hub, November 17–19. You can also talk with experts at the Azure Databricks Ask the Experts kiosk on the Expo floor, or book a meeting at The Social @ Canopy Hotel, steps from Moscone Center.

Which Microsoft Ignite 2026 sessions feature Azure Databricks?

Seven Microsoft Ignite 2026 sessions feature Azure Databricks, including the sponsored theater session "Build governed AI that understands your business context" on Wednesday, November 18, at 2:15 PM PT. Other highlights include "Powering AI with Azure Databricks, Foundry, Fabric, and Copilot" on Thursday, November 19, at 10:15 AM PT, plus sessions on Azure Government, Azure Storage, AI agents and model fine-tuning. Our guide also lists four Microsoft-led sessions on AI, agents, infrastructure and data.

What is the Azure Databricks Genie plugin for Microsoft Copilot Cowork?

The Azure Databricks Genie plugin brings governed answers from your Azure Databricks data into Microsoft Copilot Cowork, so you can ask business questions without leaving the flow of work. See it live at Booth #5271R and in the theater session on Wednesday, November 18, at 2:15 PM PT.

How can I meet with Databricks executives at Microsoft Ignite 2026?

You can request a private meeting with Databricks executives and experts at The Social @ Canopy Hotel on Tuesday, November 17, and Wednesday, November 18, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM PT. An Ignite pass isn't required to attend a meeting.
